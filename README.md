# 👋  Hi, i am MikO!

I am a senior software architect with over 20 years in experience of bringing
real software from concept to reality and in the hands of thousands of users.

As a strong advocate for data privacy and secure digital infrastructure, i know
my fair share about software security and SecOps.

I am currently *CTO* and *head of development* at [propeller GmbH][propeller]
in Trier, Germany.

In my private life, i like to work on old mopeds and hang out at my hackerspace
"[Maschinendeck e.V.][maschinendeck]", where i am currently a board member.
